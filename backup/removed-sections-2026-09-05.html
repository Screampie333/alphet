Why the name Haboob?

A haboob is a real meteorology term — a wall of dust that appears out of nowhere and swallows the sky within minutes. No long warning. It's just suddenly there.

pump.fun behaves the same way: quiet for hours, then a storm — a wild pump, or a wave of rugs. Haboob is built to catch that shift before it shows up on the chart.

Token utility

Holding isn't just speculation — it unlocks the layer underneath the public number.

  • Early accessThe daily report reaches holders 30 minutes before it's posted publicly on X.
  • Deeper dataPer-category token breakdown for higher-tier holders, not just the combined index.
  • VotingHolders vote on which token or event gets flagged as the day's "extreme condition".
  • HistoryStaking unlocks the score archive, so you can backtest how accurate the forecast has been.
