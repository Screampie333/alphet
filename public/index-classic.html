Four things Haboob reads

The weather index isn't a random number — it's combined from four on-chain signals pulled from pump.fun every hour.

Why the name Haboob?

Haboob is a real meteorology term — a massive wall of dust that appears out of nowhere and swallows the sky within minutes. No long warning, it's just suddenly there.

The pump.fun market often behaves exactly like that: quiet for hours, then suddenly a storm — either a wild pump or a wave of rugs. Haboob is built to catch that moment before the storm shows up on the chart.

Token utility

Holding the token isn't just for speculation — it unlocks access to a deeper layer of data.

  • Early accessThe daily report goes to holders 30 minutes before it's posted publicly on X.
  • Deeper dataPer-category token breakdown, not just the combined index, for higher-tier holders.
  • VotingHolders vote on which token or event deserves to be flagged as that day's "extreme condition."
  • HistoryStaking unlocks the historical score archive, so you can backtest how accurate the forecast has been.
