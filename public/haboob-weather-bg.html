Weather background

The animated sky that sits behind the report. Every layer is always mounted; a condition only moves opacity, so switching is a CSS transition rather than a rebuild.

Layers now up

How it is wired

HaboobWeatherBg.mount() inserts #weatherBg as the first child of <body>, behind everything else — the report sits at z-index:10, the dune at 1, this at 0. HaboobWeatherBg.set('storm') then moves a single data-weather attribute, and the stylesheet does the rest. On the dashboard that call rides along with the condition already being applied from each snapshot, so the sky and the index can never disagree.

Cost

Animation is limited to transform and opacity, which composite on the GPU and never trigger layout. Rain, lightning and the dust wall hold at animation-play-state:paused unless their condition is showing, so a sunny sky is not rendering rain nobody can see. Under prefers-reduced-motion the movement stops and the colours stay, because the colour is what carries the condition.
