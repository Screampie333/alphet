Alpha vs Beta

Not a price call — a quality call.

Alpha score ≥ 55

Supply is spread, liquidity can't be pulled

  • No wallet can end it. Top 10 hold a small share.
  • The pool is stuck. Liquidity burned or locked.
  • The dev has shipped before. Past launches still alive.
  • You can get out. A sell simulates clean.

Not a promise the price goes up — just the absence of what takes it to zero on purpose.

Beta score < 55

Someone else is holding the exit

  • The float is concentrated. A few wallets can end it.
  • The liquidity is withdrawable. One dev decision from empty.
  • The dev has left tokens dead. Records work both ways.
  • The exit is narrow. Mostly sells, or no sell at all.

Beta tokens do go up. That's why the money keeps arriving — and why the seam usually sits here.
